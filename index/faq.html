Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products really organic?

Yes. All our vegetables and fruits are grown without chemical pesticides or artificial fertilizers, and we check every batch before it ships.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually delivered within 24 to 48 hours depending on your city. Orders over $300 get free shipping.

What payment methods do you accept?

Currently we support Cash On Delivery. Online payment methods will be added soon.

Can I return a product?

Yes, we have a 30 day money back guarantee. If you are not happy with a product, contact us and we will fix it.

How can I track my order?

After placing your order you will get an order number. Contact our support team with it and we will give you the latest status.

How do I contact customer support?

You can use the Contact page or email us at info@webmail.com. We reply 24/7.
